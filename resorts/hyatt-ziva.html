Why we picked it

Sitting out on the tip of Punta Cancún with the Caribbean wrapping around it, Hyatt Ziva is about as water-forward as a family resort gets here. It's one of the very few in Cancún with genuine swim-up suites — step off your ground-floor terrace straight into the pool — backed by several oceanfront pool areas and a wide stretch of beach.

What sets it apart from the adults-only crowd is depth: it's a polished, amenity-rich all-inclusive that genuinely works for the whole family, and — rare for an all-inclusive — it earns and redeems World of Hyatt points. If you want a swim-up suite without committing to adults-only, this is the pick.

Who it's for

✓ Stay here if…

  • You want a real swim-up suite but not an adults-only resort.
  • You're traveling as a family and want premium amenities and kids' programming.
  • You collect World of Hyatt points and want them to count on an all-inclusive.
  • An oceanfront location on Punta Cancún matters to you.

✕ Skip it if…

  • You want strictly adults-only calm — its sister Hyatt Zilara next door is the 18+ version.
  • You're after a sprawling waterpark mega-resort — Moon Palace Grand is bigger.
  • You want the absolute top luxury tier — Le Blanc sits above it on price and polish.
