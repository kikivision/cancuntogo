Why we picked it

Sitting out on the tip of Punta Cancún with the Caribbean wrapping around it, Hyatt Ziva is about as water-forward as a family resort gets here. It's one of the very few in Cancún with genuine swim-up suites — step off your ground-floor terrace straight into the pool — backed by several oceanfront pool areas and a wide stretch of beach.

What sets it apart from the adults-only crowd is depth: it's a polished, amenity-rich all-inclusive that genuinely works for the whole family, and — rare for an all-inclusive — it earns and redeems World of Hyatt points. If you want a swim-up suite without committing to adults-only, this is the pick.

Where it sits on the Hotel Zone

Blvd.
Kukulcán

Ziva has the most unusual footprint on the strip: it occupies the Punta Cancún headland itself at Km 9.5, the pivot point where the Hotel Zone turns from its north-facing bay stretch onto the east-facing Caribbean stretch. The resort is wrapped by water on three sides.

That means it has beach on more than one aspect — a sheltered north-facing side looking across Bahía de Mujeres, and an exposed side facing the open sea. The nightlife cluster is a few minutes' walk, and the airport is roughly 20–25 minutes south.

Sargassum exposure

Low

The three-sided position is a real hedge, and it's the most underrated thing about booking Ziva in peak season. Sargassum arrives on the Atlantic drift and loads up the east-facing beaches; the sheltered north side of the headland, behind the point and facing Bahía de Mujeres, typically stays far cleaner on the same day.

So on a bad week you are not stuck with one aspect — when the ocean-facing sand is churned up, the calmer side of the property usually still works. Between roughly April and August that flexibility is worth more than any resort's cleanup schedule.

Who it's for

✓ Stay here if…

  • You want a real swim-up suite but not an adults-only resort.
  • You're traveling as a family and want premium amenities and kids' programming.
  • You collect World of Hyatt points and want them to count on an all-inclusive.
  • An oceanfront location on Punta Cancún matters to you.

✕ Skip it if…

  • You want strictly adults-only calm — its sister Hyatt Zilara, two kilometres south at Km 11.5, is the 18+ version.
  • You're after a sprawling waterpark mega-resort — Moon Palace The Grand is bigger.
  • You want the absolute top luxury tier — Le Blanc sits above it on price and polish.
