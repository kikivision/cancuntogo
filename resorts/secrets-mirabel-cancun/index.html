Why we picked it

Secrets Mirabel is the newest adults-only all-inclusive in Cancún — it opened in late 2025 — and it lands with real ambition: 487 suites strung between the Caribbean Sea and the calm Nichupté Lagoon, six pools, and eight restaurants. Where a lot of the Hotel Zone leans loud and sprawling, Mirabel is pitched as the calmer, romance-forward, design-led end of the Secrets brand.

We'll be straight with you: it's brand new, so there isn't a deep well of guest reviews yet, and a freshly opened resort is always finding its rhythm in its first months. But the bones are strong — swim-out Preferred Club suites, an agave cellar, a real focus on Mexican culture and local sourcing — and for travelers who want a fresh, modern property over a well-worn classic, it's an easy one to put on the shortlist.

Highlights

Swim-out Preferred Club suites

Step from your terrace straight into the water — plus plunge-pool and oceanfront suites in the premium Preferred Club tier.

Six pools, sea & lagoon setting

Six pools with ocean views, including two reserved for Preferred Club guests, on a strip of land between the Caribbean and Nichupté Lagoon.

Eight restaurants, no reservations

French, Mediterranean, Asian and steakhouse kitchens, a sushi bar, a wine cellar and an agave cellar — all à la carte, all included.

Culture-forward & brand-new

An agave journey through tequila and mezcal, Mexican cultural lessons, and a Secrets Spa with Mayan-inspired treatments.

Who it's for

✓ Stay here if…

  • You want a fresh, modern resort over a well-worn classic — this is the newest one on the strip.
  • A swim-out or plunge-pool suite in the Preferred Club tier is on your wish list.
  • You're a couple after a calmer, romance-forward vibe with strong food and a cultural bent.
  • You like being an early guest and don't mind a resort still hitting its stride.

✕ Skip it (for now) if…

  • You want a long track record of guest reviews — a proven pick like Secrets The Vine is the safer bet.
  • You want a lively party scene — Breathless Cancún Soul is far more social.
  • You're traveling with kids — this is adults-only; try the all-ages Hyatt Ziva instead.
  • You want the top ultra-luxury tier with butler service — Le Blanc sits above it.
