Why we picked it

Hyatt Zilara is the adults-only half of the Punta Cancún duo — same prime tip of the Hotel Zone, same Caribbean wrapping around three sides, but reserved for guests 18 and over. It's one of the rare Cancún resorts with true swim-up suites: a ground-floor terrace that steps straight into the pool, no adults-only compromise required.

Freshly renovated, it pairs a calmer, grown-up atmosphere with the amenity depth Hyatt is known for — and, unusual for an all-inclusive, it earns and redeems World of Hyatt points. If you want a refined, couples-friendly base on the beach without the kids' club soundtrack, this is the one.

Where it sits on the Hotel Zone

Blvd.
Kukulcán

Zilara sits at Km 11.5, on one of the widest stretches of sand in the Hotel Zone. Worth being clear about this, because the two Cancún Hyatts are often assumed to be neighbours: Zilara is not at Punta Cancún — that's Ziva, about two kilometres north. Zilara is squarely on the east-facing Caribbean side.

The upside of being south of the point is width and quiet; the downside is that the nightlife cluster is now a taxi rather than a walk. La Isla Shopping Village is about a kilometre south, which is walkable. Airport, roughly 20 minutes.

Sargassum exposure

Moderate

East-facing at Km 11.5 puts Zilara in the middle band. It sits far enough south of the Punta Cancún headland to lose the shelter that keeps Coral Beach and the north-side beaches clear, but north of the Km 14–20 stretch that takes the heaviest load between April and August.

The very wide beach here does help in one practical way: even when a band of sargassum comes ashore and the crews are working it, there's usually more usable dry sand left over than on a narrower stretch. Expect some bad days in peak season and plan the pools as the fallback.

Who it's for

✓ Stay here if…

  • You want a real swim-up suite in an adults-only setting.
  • You're after a calm, couples-friendly all-inclusive on the beach.
  • You collect World of Hyatt points and want them to count on an all-inclusive.
  • An oceanfront location on Punta Cancún matters to you.

✕ Skip it if…

  • You're traveling with kids — its sister Hyatt Ziva next door is the all-ages version.
  • You want a high-energy party scene — Breathless Cancún Soul leans more social.
  • You want the absolute top luxury tier — Le Blanc sits above it on price and polish.
