The best time to visit Cancún

Cancún is warm and beautiful all year — but weather, crowds, prices, and hurricane season swing a lot by month. Here's how to pick the window that fits your trip.

The short answer

Best windows to book

Aim for these

  • Late Nov – mid-Dec: the sweet spot — dry, calm, pre-holiday prices.
  • January – February: prime sunny, dry weather (peak prices though).
  • May – early June: warm and good value before the summer rush.
Go in with eyes open

Trade-offs to weigh

  • Sep – Oct: cheapest of all, but peak hurricane season.
  • Holidays & March: priciest and most crowded (spring break).
  • May – Aug: sargassum (seaweed) is most likely to show up.
If you want one answer
Book late November to mid-December — dry-season weather is settling in, hurricane season is over, and you beat the holiday crowds and prices.

Two seasons, not four

Cancún really has two seasons. The dry season (roughly December to April) is the postcard version: sunny, low humidity, highs in the low-to-mid 80s°F (around 28–31°C), and calm, clear water. It's also the busiest and most expensive — this is high season.

The wet season (May to October) is hotter and much more humid, with short, heavy afternoon storms and warmer seas. It overlaps with hurricane season and the months sargassum is most likely. The upside: fewer crowds and noticeably lower prices. The shoulder weeks on either side — late April–May and November–early December — are where many travelers find the best balance.

The four things that move the needle

Weather & humidity

December through April is the most comfortable stretch — sunny, dry, and far less humid, with warm days and pleasant evenings. From May the heat and humidity ramp up and short afternoon thunderstorms become routine, clearing as fast as they arrive. The sea is warm enough to swim in all year.

Best: Dec – Apr

Crowds & holidays

The biggest crushes are Christmas–New Year, March spring break, and Easter week — busy beaches, packed restaurants, and top dollar. September and October are the quietest. For thinner crowds with good weather, target November–early December or the May shoulder.

Busiest: holidays, March, Easter

Prices

Rates track demand closely. Expect the highest prices over the winter holidays and through the dry-season peak. The cheapest fares and resort rates land in September–October, with early May to mid-June a close second — warm weather at low-season pricing, just before the summer family rush.

Cheapest: Sep – Oct

Hurricanes & sargassum

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1–November 30, peaking August–October (September most of all). A direct hit is uncommon, but it's worth travel insurance in those months. Separately, sargassum seaweed is most likely from about April through August. If clear water and calm seas are the priority, the late-November-through-March window is your friend.

Watch: Jun – Nov storms · Apr – Aug sargassum

Common questions

What is the best month to visit Cancún?

For the best balance, late November to mid-December is hard to beat — dry-season weather is settling in, hurricane season has ended, and you beat the holiday crowds and prices. January and February have the most reliable sunshine if you don't mind peak pricing.

When is hurricane season in Cancún?

June 1 through November 30, with the highest risk from August to October and September the peak. Direct hits are uncommon, but storms can bring rain, rough seas, and travel disruption — many travelers add insurance for trips in these months.

What is the cheapest time to visit Cancún?

September and October are cheapest, falling in the low season and peak hurricane window. Early May to mid-June is the next-best value, with warm weather and lower rates before the summer family rush.

When is sargassum season?

Sargassum (seaweed) is most likely from about April through August, often peaking May to July. It varies a lot by year and by beach. For the clearest water, the late-November-through-March window tends to be best. See our month-by-month sargassum guide →

Is Cancún always hot?

Yes — it's warm year-round. Winter (December–April) is the most comfortable: sunny, dry, highs in the low-to-mid 80s°F with cooler evenings. Summer (May–October) is hotter and far more humid, with afternoon storms common.
