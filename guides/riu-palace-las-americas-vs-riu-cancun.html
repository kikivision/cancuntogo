Same beach, different volume

Riu runs two adults-only resorts side by side at Punta Cancún — Riu Palace Las Americas at Km 8.5 and Hotel Riu Cancún at Km 9 — close enough to walk between in a few minutes. That makes this an unusual comparison, because the thing that usually decides a Cancún booking is off the table.

Both sit on the north-facing bay side of the Punta Cancún headland. Both get the same calm, swimmable water. Both get the same sargassum protection — which is to say, the best odds on the strip. The beach is a tie, and anyone telling you otherwise is describing the same sand twice.

What's left is tier and volume. Las Americas is the Palace one: Riu's upper brand, 372 junior suites and suites, quieter. Riu Cancún is the bigger one: roughly 569 rooms, four pools, and the Pacha discotheque on site and included in the rate. One of those is a selling point and one is a dealbreaker, depending entirely on who you are.

How they actually differ

The beach is a tie — genuinely

Worth saying plainly, because most comparisons of these two invent a difference here. They are 500 metres apart on the same north-facing stretch behind the Punta Cancún headland: same calm water, same protection from the Atlantic rafts that close the east-facing beaches in peak season. If sargassum is what's driving your booking, either one is among the best choices in Cancún, and you should decide on something else.

No edge — same sand

Tier and size

Las Americas is the Palace property: 372 junior suites and suites, three pools, seven restaurants and a full-service spa, with the round-the-clock service and in-room liquor dispensers Riu is known for. Riu Cancún is a substantially bigger resort at roughly 569 rooms, with four pools and six restaurants. Bigger means more choice and more energy; it also means more people at the pool at eleven in the morning.

Edge: Palace on calm, Riu Cancún on scale

The nightclub

Riu Cancún has the Pacha discotheque on the property and included in the rate. This is the clearest difference between the two and it is entirely polarising: it is either the reason to book Riu Cancún or the reason to book next door. Las Americas is a five-minute walk from the Punta Cancún nightlife cluster, so choosing the Palace doesn't mean giving up the option — it means not sleeping above it.

Edge: whichever way you lean

Adults-only, both — and that surprises people

Hotel Riu Cancún ran as a family resort for years and a lot of older advice still describes it that way. It is adults-only, 18 and over, and so is Las Americas. If you are travelling with children neither of these is the answer, and you want the family section of the list instead.

No edge — but check it before you book

Price

Riu Cancún is usually the cheaper of the two, sometimes by a wide margin, and you get a larger resort for it. The Palace premium buys a quieter property, suite-grade rooms and upper-tier service — not a better beach and not a better location. Whether that's worth it is a taste question rather than a value one.

Edge: Riu Cancún, on value

The verdict

Want quiet, and suite-grade rooms? Book Riu Palace Las Americas. You're paying for a smaller, calmer resort in the upper Riu tier — and the nightlife is still a five-minute walk when you want it.

Want the scene, and the lower price? Book Hotel Riu Cancún. Bigger, louder, cheaper, with a club on site and included in the rate.

Whichever you pick, you get the same calm, well-protected Punta Cancún beach — which is the reason to be looking at either of them in the first place.

Common questions

Are Riu Palace Las Americas and Hotel Riu Cancún the same hotel?

No. They are two separate Riu properties next door to each other, at Blvd. Kukulcán Km 8.5 and Km 9 respectively. Both are adults-only.

Is Hotel Riu Cancún adults-only?

Yes, 18 and over. It operated as a family resort in the past and some older guides still describe it that way, but it now takes adults only.

Which has less seaweed?

Neither — they sit on the same north-facing stretch behind the Punta Cancún headland, roughly 500 metres apart, and share the same low sargassum exposure. It is among the best-protected sand in Cancún.

Which is cheaper?

Hotel Riu Cancún is usually the cheaper of the two. Riu Palace Las Americas is Riu's upper tier and prices above it.

Does Riu Cancún have a nightclub?

Yes. The Pacha discotheque is on the property and included in the all-inclusive rate. Riu Palace Las Americas does not have one, but is about five minutes' walk from the Punta Cancún nightlife cluster.
