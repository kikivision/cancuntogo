Two addresses, two different vacations

First, a correction to something almost every comparison of these two gets wrong: they are not next door to each other. Hyatt Ziva occupies the Punta Cancún headland at Km 9.5, wrapped by water on three sides. Hyatt Zilara is about two kilometres south at Km 11.5, on the open-Caribbean stretch. Same brand, same swim-up suites, same World of Hyatt points — but two genuinely different addresses.

The obvious decision between them is simple: Ziva is all-ages and family-friendly; Zilara is strictly adults-only (18+). If you're travelling in sargassum season, though, that two-kilometre gap is the second question worth asking — see Location & beach below. They're run as two separate resorts, so plan to spend your stay enjoying the one you book.

How they actually differ

Atmosphere & crowd

This is the real difference. Ziva carries the buzz of a family resort — kids in the pools, programming, a fuller, livelier soundtrack. Zilara is the quiet one: adults only, so the pools and beach stay calm and the bars skew date-night. If kid-free peace is the point of the trip, Zilara wins; if you want your kids entertained and welcome, Ziva does.

Depends on your group

Rooms & swim-up suites

Both deliver the headline feature: genuine swim-up suites where your ground-floor terrace opens straight onto the pool. Zilara's rooms benefit from a recent renovation and a more grown-up finish, while Ziva offers larger family-friendly configurations. For couples wanting the freshest, most polished room, Zilara has a slight edge.

Slight edge: Zilara

Location & beach

Not a tie — this is the most under-reported difference between them. Ziva sits on the Punta Cancún headland at Km 9.5, the pivot point where the Hotel Zone turns from its sheltered north-facing bay onto the open Caribbean. It has beach on three sides, including a north-facing aspect tucked behind the point. Zilara is two kilometres south at Km 11.5, squarely east-facing — on one of the widest stretches of sand on the strip, but with no shelter from the open sea.

In sargassum season (roughly April–August) that matters more than anything else on this page. Sargassum rides in from the east, so Ziva's sheltered side typically stays far cleaner on the same day that Zilara's beach is being raked — and when one aspect at Ziva is churned up, you can walk to another. Zilara's compensation is width: even on a bad day there's usually more dry sand left over than on a narrower stretch. Full sargassum season breakdown →

Ziva is also the more walkable base, with the Punta Cancún nightlife and shopping cluster a few minutes away; from Zilara that's a taxi, though La Isla Shopping Village is about a kilometre south. Both run roughly 20 minutes from the airport.

Edge: Ziva — sheltered beach, more walkable

Hyatt points & value

Both earn and redeem World of Hyatt points — the standout perk versus most Cancún all-inclusives, and a strong reason to pick this pair if you're loyal to Hyatt. Pricing runs close; book whichever fits your dates and group, since the points math is the same on both.

Tie

The verdict

Traveling with kids? Book Hyatt Ziva. It's purpose-built to keep children entertained and the grown-ups happy at the same time, without giving up the swim-up suite or the Hyatt points.

Kid-free trip — couple, friends, or honeymoon? Book Hyatt Zilara. You get the same address and swim-up suites in a calmer, freshly renovated, adults-only setting.

Common questions

Are Hyatt Ziva and Hyatt Zilara the same resort?

No — and they are not next door either. Ziva sits on the Punta Cancún headland at Blvd. Kukulcán Km 9.5; Zilara is about two kilometres south at Km 11.5. They share a brand and design language but operate as two separate all-inclusive resorts. Ziva is all-ages and family-friendly; Zilara is strictly adults-only (18+).

Which is better for families?

Hyatt Ziva. It's the all-ages resort, with kids' programming and family amenities. Hyatt Zilara is adults-only and does not accept guests under 18.

Do both have swim-up suites?

Yes. Both offer true swim-up suites — ground-floor rooms with terraces that open directly onto the pool — which is rare in Cancún.

Do both earn World of Hyatt points?

Yes. Both are part of the Hyatt portfolio and let you earn and redeem World of Hyatt points, which is unusual for all-inclusive resorts.
